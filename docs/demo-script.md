# PranaPulse demo script (3 minutes)

Start the app, sign in, and have the demo patients loaded (Files tab, "Try demo patients").

| Time | Do this | Say this |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Open **My summary** | "PranaPulse compares each person with their own earlier self, never with a population average." |
| 0:25 | Click the **Weight, sugar and activity** card | "This area shows a change worth a closer look: several related measures moved the same way over the year." |
| 0:50 | Open **Trends**, pick a measure, switch the range, turn on the projection | "The chart shows the person's own baseline and an 8-week straight-line projection. It is a continuation of the trend, not a prediction." |
| 1:20 | Open **Compare**, pick two measures | "Related measures can be viewed side by side to see whether they moved together." |
| 1:45 | Open **Ask a question** (or the chat button) and ask "Why was this flagged?" | "Answers come from the person's own readings. A local model only rewords them, and every reply is checked for safe wording and for numbers that are not in the data." |
| 2:15 | Ask "Do I have diabetes?" | "Safety questions never reach the model. The tool does not diagnose, rate risk or advise on treatment." |
| 2:35 | Open **My files**, show where readings are added | "Everything stays on this computer; the person can delete their data at any time." |
| 2:50 | Switch to light or dark mode | "The interface adapts to the person's preference." |

Doctor / healthcare provider accounts also have: **All patients**, **Full findings** (grouped into cardiovascular, metabolic and women's health), **Data quality**, and **Activity log**.

Points to land: per-person baseline, explainable evidence, honest data-sufficiency flags, no diagnosis, runs locally.
