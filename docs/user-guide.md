# PranaPulse: a short guide for patients

PranaPulse looks at your own health readings and shows where something has moved away from your usual pattern. It highlights patterns that may be worth discussing with a healthcare provider. It does not diagnose or give medical advice.

## 1. Add your readings
Open **My files**. Upload your workbook (.xlsx), or choose **Try demo patients** to explore with example data. Your files stay on this computer and you can delete them at any time.

## 2. Read your summary
**My summary** shows three health areas: heart and blood pressure, weight, sugar and activity, and cycle and hormones. Each card says either **No clear change** or **Changes found**, with a plain sentence about what moved. Click a card to see the evidence.

## 3. Look at trends
Open **Trends**. Pick a measure, change the date range, and turn on the projection to see your trend carried forward 8 weeks. The shaded band shows where readings would usually land. The projection is not a prediction.

## 4. Compare two measures
Open **Compare** and pick two measures to see on one chart whether they moved together.

## 5. Ask a question
Open **Ask a question**, or use the round chat button at the bottom right. Ask things like "Why was this flagged?" or "How is my weight?". Answers come from your own readings. Questions about diagnosis, medicines or treatment are not answered; ask a healthcare provider instead.

## Tips
- The **?** button opens a plain-language guide to the terms.
- The moon or sun button switches between light and dark mode.
- If a result worries you, talk to a healthcare provider.
